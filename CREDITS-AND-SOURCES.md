## Evidence and limitations to carry into the pitch

- NASA POWER historical grid records are not real-time in-field measurements.
- SMAP, IMERG and MODIS visual layers are **not sampled into irrigation or suitability calculations**.
- Open-Meteo forecast is not a NASA prediction.
- Economics is scenario analysis, not measured profit or a yield forecast.
- Current code does not output evidence-backed crop rotation sequences; crop suitability is unresolved pending field data and an approved method.
- Any 30–40% water-saving target is a **hypothesis** unless independently validated; not an actual result.
