QUICK DEMO
1) Choose a real location or coordinates.
2) Reports -> Load NASA climate context (past-year historical NASA POWER grids).
3) Crop planner -> select a current crop, local water access, optional measured pH, and priority -> Compare paths.
4) Review three illustrative crop-family pathways and the provenance/limitations.
5) Add a dated soil record if available; enter your own price/cost/yield assumptions and export a report.

DATA HONESTY
- NASA POWER: historical regional-grid climate information, NOT live farm sensors or near-term weather forecasts.
- NASA GIBS: visible map imagery (SMAP, IMERG, MODIS, SRTM), NOT numerical field estimates in crop/irrigation calculations.
- Open-Meteo: current modeled weather + short forecast; NOT NASA weather.
- Crop pathways: exploratory family-diversity sequences, NOT validated planting/irrigation recommendations.
- Profit: scenarios calculated from user-entered values; NOT real observed farm profit.
- Source code and tests include documented 14 Nov 2026 ChatGPT assistance.
- Media licenses, SI watermarks and the old 8-slide concept PDF require team review: see CREDITS-AND-SOURCES.md.
